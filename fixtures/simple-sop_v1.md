# SOP-QA-017 Deviation Management

Effective date: 01 March 2026. Supersedes revision 3.

## 1. Purpose

This procedure defines the process for recording, investigating and closing deviations from approved manufacturing and quality control procedures at the site. It applies to planned and unplanned deviations affecting product quality, process consistency or data integrity.

Deviations relating to clinical trial material are covered by SOP-CT-004 and are outside the scope of this procedure.

## 2. Responsibilities

The **Quality Assurance Manager** shall review all deviations within five working days of notification and determine the classification. The classification decision shall be documented in the deviation record together with its justification.

- Area Supervisor: raise the deviation record and secure any affected material.
- Investigation Owner: complete the root cause investigation and propose corrective actions.
## 3. Procedure

Any employee who identifies a deviation shall notify their supervisor immediately and, in any case, before the end of the shift on which the deviation was identified.

The Area Supervisor shall raise a deviation record in the quality management system within one working day. The record shall describe what happened, when it was identified, which batches or systems are affected, and what immediate action was taken to contain the issue.

Deviations classified as *critical* shall be escalated to the Site Head and to the Qualified Person on the day of classification. A critical deviation shall not be closed without a documented product impact assessment.

Corrective and preventive actions shall be assigned an owner and a target date. Extension of a target date requires the written approval of the Quality Assurance Manager and a justification recorded against the action.

Deviation records shall be retained for the shelf life of the affected product plus one year.
